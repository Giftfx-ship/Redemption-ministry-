About Redemption Ministries

Our story, vision, and mission

Our Story

Who We Are

Redemption Ministries Warri is a Bible-believing church, located in the heart of Warri, Delta State.

We are committed to raising young and older generations, preparing them for the Master's use.

"But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay... If anyone cleanses himself from the latter, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified and useful for the Master's use, prepared for every good work." — 2 Timothy 2:20-21

Redemption Ministries was brought to Warri with the aim of fulfilling the Scriptures.

"Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations..." — Matthew 28:19-20

And sharing the good news of our Lord Jesus Christ.

"For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes..." — Romans 1:16

Our Calling

We are also called to bring deliverance to the oppressed.

"The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me, because He has anointed Me to preach the gospel to the poor; He has sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives... to set at liberty those who are oppressed." — Luke 4:18

And to heal the sick, raise the dead, cast out demons.

"Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out demons. Freely you have received, freely give." — Matthew 10:8
What We Believe

Our Core Values

Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our final authority for faith and practice.

Prayer

We believe prayer changes things. We are committed to a lifestyle of persistent prayer.

Love

We believe love is the greatest commandment. We love God and love people.

Service

We believe we are saved to serve. We are committed to serving our community.

Excellence

We believe in doing everything with excellence as unto the Lord.

Passion

We believe in passionate worship and fervent spirit.
