About Redemption Ministries

Our story, vision, and mission

Our Story

Who We Are

Redemption Ministries Warri is a vibrant church family located in the heart of Warri, Delta State. We are committed to raising a redeemed generation that walks in the authority of the firstborn.

Our church was founded with a vision to reach souls with the gospel of Jesus Christ. Over the years, we have grown into a family of believers who are passionate about worship, prayer, and the Word of God.

We serve as a beacon of hope to our community and beyond.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our final authority for faith and practice.

Prayer

We believe prayer changes things. We are committed to a lifestyle of persistent prayer.

Love

We believe love is the greatest commandment. We love God and love people.

Service

We believe we are saved to serve. We are committed to serving our community.

Excellence

We believe in doing everything with excellence as unto the Lord.

Passion

We believe in passionate worship and fervent spirit.
