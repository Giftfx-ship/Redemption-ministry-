The Path to Redemption

Believe

“Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved.” — Acts 16:31

Repent

“Repent therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out.” — Acts 3:19

Turn away from sin, turn toward God. A changed heart produces new life.

“If you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus... you shall be saved.” — Romans 10:9–10

Openly declare Jesus as your Lord and become a new creation.
