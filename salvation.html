Steps to Salvation

Begin your journey with these foundational principles

The Path

Three Simple Steps

Believe

"For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life."

— John 3:16

Repent

"Repent therefore and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, so that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord."

— Acts 3:19

Confess

"If you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God has raised Him from the dead, you will be saved."

— Romans 10:9

Ready to Give Your Life to Christ?

Say this prayer from your heart and believe in Jesus Christ. He will forgive your sins and give you eternal life.

"Lord Jesus, I believe You died for my sins. I repent of my sins and confess You as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for saving me. Amen."
